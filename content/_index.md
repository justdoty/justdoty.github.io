I'm an economist and senior application engineer at [MathWorks](https://www.mathworks.com/), where I help economists at central banks, government agencies, and financial institutions build and scale econometric models in MATLAB.

Before MathWorks, I was a research economist at the [U.S. Census Bureau](https://www.census.gov/) (2022–2026). There I developed [differentially private](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Differential_privacy) methods for releasing business and survey data, including algorithms for heavy-tailed establishment-level data, and co-authored work published in the [*Journal of Privacy and Confidentiality*](https://doi.org/10.29012/jpc.992).

My research is in econometrics and empirical industrial organization, with a focus on production function estimation under unobserved heterogeneity and measurement error. I received my Ph.D. in Economics from the [University of Iowa](https://tippie.uiowa.edu/about/economics-department) in 2022, where I also taught econometrics and microeconomics.

If you're working on econometric modeling in MATLAB, feel free to [get in touch](#contact).
